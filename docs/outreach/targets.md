## Selection rule
1. T01 is Tune Up Research Desk (the only real report seller found; reports are Markdown, so the Markdown-to-JSONL route in the seller guide applies).

5. At most 15, ordered Tune Up first, then data-set publishers, then research writers. Follower count is not a criterion. Contact is a seller invitation, never a partnership offer.
